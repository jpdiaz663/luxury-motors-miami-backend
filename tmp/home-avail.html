Important information

Conditions before you take the keys, and the questions we answer at pickup.

  • Rental conditions

    • Be over 21 years old (minors under 25 years old pay coinsurance).
    • Identity document.
    • Current driving license.
    • Credit card (own or a guarantor).
    • Minimum rental duration 48 hours.

  • Clarifications

    • Reservations will be served with cars of the same category or, if there is no availability, a vehicle of a higher category will be delivered.
    • No reservation request is valid without confirmation from Rently via reservation number.
    • The rented vehicle may only be driven by the owner of the rental contract, or whoever has been previously designated by Rently.
    • The return of the car is contemplated with 1 (one) hour of tolerance for its return. After the tolerance hour, ½ day of rental will be charged. After 5 hours, a full day of rental will be charged.

  • Unit delivery

    • Rently delivers its vehicles to its headquarters at 8 the green, Dover, Delaware, USA.
    • In the case of arranging delivery to your home, Rently will add the DROP OFF costs to the cost of renting the unit.
    • The vehicles are delivered with a full tank and must be returned in the same way.
    • Prices may be modified without prior notice. To freeze the price of a reservation, full payment of the rental is required.

  • Payment methods

    • You can pay with Visa, Visa Electron, Mastercard, Maestro, American Express, Cabal, bank transfer, check and/or cash.
    • Rentals are paid in advance, either in cash or with any other form of payment.
    • All prices include 21% VAT and insurance.
What are the requirements for renting a car from your company?

You must be over 21. Drivers under 25 pay coinsurance. Bring an identity document, a current driving license, and a credit card in your name or a guarantor's. The minimum rental duration is 48 hours.

How do I make a reservation?

Send a reservation request and wait for confirmation from Rently with a reservation number. No request is valid without that confirmation.

What types of cars do you offer for rent?

Vehicles are assigned by category. Your reservation is served with a car in the same category or, if none is available, a vehicle of a higher category.

Can I modify or cancel my reservation?

Changes need a confirmed reservation number. Contact the desk with that number before pickup to modify or cancel.

Is insurance included with my rental?

Yes. All prices include 21% VAT and insurance.

What documents do I need to present when picking up the rental car?

Identity document, current driving license, and a credit card (yours or a guarantor's).

Are there any mileage restrictions on rental cars?

Mileage terms are confirmed with your reservation number. Ask the desk when the booking is confirmed.

What should I do in case of an emergency or breakdown?

Contact the rental desk immediately and follow the instructions in your contract. Do not leave the vehicle unattended without guidance from Rently.

Can I use the rental car to go out of Florida?

Only the contract holder, or a driver previously designated by Rently, may drive the vehicle. Out-of-state travel must be confirmed in writing with your reservation.
